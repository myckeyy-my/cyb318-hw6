HOW QUALIFYING WORKS

  • Each level provides five attempts.
  • Complete valid laps and keep the fastest time.
  • Beat the target to qualify.
  • After qualifying, improve your time or choose Next Round.

VALID / INVALID LAPS

VALID LAP

Full lap, no barrier or obstacle hit, and all four wheels stay within the permitted surface.

INVALID LAP

Obstacle/barrier hit or all four wheels leave the track. The lap is discarded and one attempt is consumed.

Target 50.000 s · Valid 52.000 s = valid lap, but not qualified. A lap must be strictly faster.
